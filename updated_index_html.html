About Our Ministry

A Heart Dedicated to Prayer

Marsil Pray Team is a Christian prayer community committed to seeking God, strengthening faith, supporting one another, praying for families and communities, and sharing the message of Jesus Christ.

We believe in the transformative power of persistent prayer and the unfailing love of our Lord. Gathered together in unity and spiritual devotion, our ministry serves as a beacon of hope, intercession, and fellowship for believers everywhere.

Whether you are seeking spiritual renewal, comfort in times of trial, or a dedicated community to stand with you in agreement, our doors and hearts are always open.

Our Vision

To build a vibrant global community united in faith, continuous prayer, and spiritual growth.

Our Mission

To encourage believers through powerful prayer gatherings, uplifting worship, and authentic Christian fellowship.

Our Faith

To follow Jesus Christ wholeheartedly, living and preaching according to the infallible truth of God's Word.
